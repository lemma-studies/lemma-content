This is a study copy, not a final tome. It is a working document
offered to the church for community discernment on a question
Scripture treats with more silence than most of us are comfortable
with — the question of what parent-and-adult-child life should look
like *between* the end of childhood and marriage.

**Who this is written for.** Christian households and congregations
wrestling with this question — parents and adult children who share
the confession that Scripture is authoritative, that Christ is Lord,
and that the Church's historic witness on family and marriage is
worth listening to before departing from it. The paper is not
written to persuade a secular reader; where a "warmly affectionate
secular family" appears in Chapter 5, it is a diagnostic
thought-experiment, not the intended audience.

What this is:

- **A structured SCAR walk** through Scripture, Christocentrism,
  Antiquity, and Reality on the question, presenting each faithful
  Christian view at its steel-manned best, drawn where possible from
  its own advocates' published words.
- **A reader-training document.** The final chapter teaches the
  reader to walk their own case through SCAR before the Lord, in
  prayer and in community.
- **A shared-ground close.** Where the views agree, they agree on
  Christ Himself as treasure and goal. That is not view-picking; it
  is naming the ground beneath the views.

What this is not:

- Not a personal manifesto. The author is named (see the Attribution
  note below), but no faithful view is commended in the narrator's
  voice; the argument stands or falls on its evidence, not the byline.
- Not a settled ecclesial statement. It does not bind any household,
  congregation, or tradition.
- Not a polemic against any of the views. Every view named as
  faithful is presented as its best advocates present it. Section
  5.5 names two paired *failure modes* — the Clutch and the
  Abandonment — that can afflict any household drifting toward its
  view's idolatry rather than its view's central good. The failure
  modes are distinguished from the doctrines they can distort, and
  the distinction between doctrine and drift is drawn at every
  point where the modes appear.
- Not neutral about *everything.* Where Scripture speaks clearly on
  the *what* of the question — that honor the father and the mother
  is a lifelong command with content (Ex 20:12; Matt 15:4-6; Eph
  6:2-3), that no tradition may forbid marriage (1 Tim 4:1-3), that
  no tradition may nullify honor (Mark 7:9-13) — the paper is
  faithful, not neutral. Where Scripture leaves the *how* to
  household judgment, the paper presents the honest disagreement
  and trains the reader in the tools rather than adjudicating a
  winner.

The reader is invited to sit with the material, walk the case
through SCAR before the Lord, and bring the fruit into their own
household and congregation for prayer and counsel.

## On Attribution, Citation, and Correspondence

**Attribution.** This paper is written by **Tim Uy**. Earlier drafts
were issued as a corporate work without a name, on the reasoning that
the argument should be weighed on its merits and not on any author's
personal reputation. That discipline has done what it was meant to do
during drafting; the paper is now offered under the author's own name
so that the reader can address the writer directly, and so that
credit or blame for what is here lands where it belongs. The argument
itself still stands or falls on the evidence — the exegesis, the
patristic and Reformation apparatus, the historical record — not on
the byline.

**Suggested citation.** Cite by author, title, and version, e.g.:

> Tim Uy, *Parents and Adult Children Before Marriage: A SCAR
> Quadrilateral Analysis*, Draft v2.16.0 (2026-08-04).

For a specific passage: Tim Uy, *Parents and Adult Children Before
Marriage: A SCAR Quadrilateral Analysis*, Draft v2.16.0 (2026-08-04),
§1.1.1 (four senses of authority).

**Version-and-date convention.** Every substantive revision bumps the
minor or major version and the date on the title page moves with it
(so v2.7.1 dated 2026-07-22 is a distinct citable object from v2.6.2
dated 2026-07-22 even where the day is the same). Version numbers
never move backward; a citation to Draft v2.7.1 will always point to
the same text.

**Correspondence.** Criticisms, encouragements, and exhortations are
welcome at **torque@gmail.com**. The paper is issued through and to
communities capable of weighing it — pastors, elders, seminary
readers, and Christian households — who are asked to test its
exegesis against Scripture, its historical claims against the primary
sources named in Appendix B, and its pastoral claims against their
own lived experience of parents and adult children before marriage.
Corrections, counter-evidence, and pastoral pushback are welcome; the
version number exists precisely so that such feedback can be received
and integrated into a subsequent draft that succeeds this one on the
citable record.
