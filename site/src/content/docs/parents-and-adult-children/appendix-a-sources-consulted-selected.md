## Scripture

King James Version, throughout.

## Deuterocanonical / Second-Temple Sources

The paper cites two deuterocanonical books as *Second-Temple
background* rather than as canonical Scripture-leg witnesses,
consistent with Article 6 of the Thirty-Nine Articles and Westminster
Confession 1.3, both of which distinguish the Apocrypha from the
canonical books while permitting their use "for example of life and
instruction of manners" (Article 6). Editions cited:

- *The Wisdom of Ben Sira* (Sirach 3:1-16, 3:12-16 quoted in §2.5 as
  Second-Temple background to Mark 7 korban). The quoted block
  follows the *KJV Apocrypha* (per the 2026-07-24 source
  verification); the *Revised Standard Version — Second Catholic
  Edition* (Ignatius Press, 2006) and the *Douay-Rheims* (DRA) are
  consulted for comparison where noted.
- *Tobit* (Tobit 4-11 summarized in §4.5 for the arrangement pattern;
  6:11-13; 7:11-14; 8:6 wedding prayer; 10-11 return blessing). Same
  editions.

## Pre-Nicene Sources

- *Shepherd of Hermas*, Visions 1-2, quoted from the ANF vol. 2
  translation (Roberts and Donaldson, 1885); Greek text and
  modern translation in Bart D. Ehrman, *The Apostolic Fathers*
  vol. 2, Loeb Classical Library 25 (Harvard, 2003); older Loeb
  edition Kirsopp Lake, 1912-13; commentary in Carolyn Osiek,
  *Shepherd of Hermas*, Hermeneia (Fortress, 1999).
- *First Epistle of Clement to the Corinthians* 21:6-8, same
  editions.
- Justin Martyr, *First Apology* 15, quoted from ANF vol. 1
  (Roberts and Donaldson, 1885)
- Athenagoras, *Legatio pro Christianis* 33, quoted from ANF
  vol. 2 (Pratten, 1885)
- Clement of Alexandria, *Paedagogus* (esp. III.11) and *Stromata*
  III.12.79-90. English in ANF vol. 2 (Wilson, 1885). Critical
  Greek text and French translation in Sources chrétiennes 70,
  108, 158 (Mondésert, Marrou, Matray, Van den Hoek). English of
  *Stromata* Book III with commentary in John Ferguson, *Clement
  of Alexandria: Stromateis Books One to Three,* Fathers of the
  Church 85 (CUA Press, 1991).
- Tertullian, *On the Veiling of Virgins* 1 and 11 (quoted from
  ANF vol. 4, Thelwall trans.); *Ad Uxorem* I.5-8 (summarized,
  not quoted)
- *Passio Perpetuae et Felicitatis.* English rendering after W. H.
  Shewring, *The Passion of SS. Perpetua and Felicity, MM.*
  (Sheed & Ward, 1931); critical Latin/Greek text in Thomas J.
  Heffernan, *The Passion of Perpetua and Felicity* (Oxford, 2012);
  earlier critical edition C. J. M. J. van Beek, *Passio Sanctarum
  Perpetuae et Felicitatis* (Nijmegen, 1936).
- Eusebius of Caesarea, *Historia Ecclesiastica* 6.2 (Origen's youth).
  Kirsopp Lake, LCL 265 (Harvard, 1932); English also in NPNF² vol. 1
  (Schaff, 1890).
- Cyprian, *De Habitu Virginum* 3 and 22, quoted from ANF vol. 5
  (Wallis, 1886); *Epistle 4* (modern critical numbering; = ANF
  *Epistle 61*, "To Pomponius, Concerning Some Virgins") on the
  *virgines subintroductae*
- Methodius, *Symposium* (Banquet of the Ten Virgins)

*Note on patristic editions.* Where the paper quotes pre-Nicene
sources in English, the rendering follows the Ante-Nicene Fathers
translation (Roberts and Donaldson, eds., 1885-1887) unless a more
recent scholarly translation is named at the quotation point (as
with the Shewring rendering of the *Passio* in §3.7.1). Chapter and
section divisions follow the standard critical editions.

## Modern Sources — The Arrangement Question (Parental Mediation of the Marriage Covenant)

- Wilson, Doug. *Her Hand in Marriage: Biblical Courtship in the
  Modern World.* Canon Press, 1997.
- Harris, Joshua. *I Kissed Dating Goodbye.* Multnomah, 1997.
  Formally retracted by the author in October 2018 (statement at
  joshharris.com), disowning the book's central thesis; cited here
  as a historical influence on the courtship revival, not as a
  currently-advocated position.
- Cover, Dwight. *Marrying, Giving in Marriage, and the Will of
  God.* Equip08 NW Conference lecture (2008); expanded lecture
  notes and full-text edition, 2010s. Develops the threefold
  Seek → Betroth → Take pattern, the supra-cultural argument, the
  bride-price grammar, and the redemptive-history flow used in
  §5.3.
- Cover, Dwight. *Reflections on Birth Control and the Will of God.*
  Undated process paper. Applies the Precept → Principle → Person
  of Truth hermeneutic to the fertility question; models the
  leaky-bucket cumulative-argument method and the assumptive-language
  audit used in §6.3.
- Ignatius of Antioch, *To Polycarp* 5:1-2 (c. 107 AD). The pre-
  Nicene ecclesial-marriage warrant.
- Orthodox Jewish *shidduch* halakhic literature on matchmaking and
  family responsibility in Jewish marriage formation.
- Historic Roman Catholic canon law on marriage formation: Council
  of Trent, Session 24 (1563); 1983 Code of Canon Law, canons
  1058-1071.

## Historical and Sociological Sources (Appendix B)

- Hajnal, John. "European Marriage Patterns in Perspective." In D. V.
  Glass and D. E. C. Eversley, eds., *Population in History,* Arnold,
  1965. The founding statement of the Northwest European Marriage
  Pattern used in §B.2.1.
- Kussmaul, Ann. *Servants in Husbandry in Early Modern England.*
  Cambridge University Press, 1981. Chapter 1 for the ~60 percent
  service figure cited in §B.2.1.
- Laslett, Peter, and Richard Wall, eds. *Household and Family in
  Past Time.* Cambridge University Press, 1972. Cambridge Group
  household-structure documentation.
- Morgan, Edmund S. *The Puritan Family: Religion and Domestic
  Relations in Seventeenth-Century New England.* Public Affairs
  Press, 1944; revised edition, Harper & Row, 1966. Cited in §B.2.1
  for Puritan household legal regulation of service placements.
- Whitmore, William H., ed. *The Colonial Laws of Massachusetts,
  Reprinted from the Edition of 1672, with the Supplements through
  1686.* Boston: Rockwell and Churchill, 1889. Primary-source
  reprint for the 1668 Massachusetts Bay orders cited in §B.2.1.
- Kraybill, Donald B. *The Riddle of Amish Culture.* Revised edition,
  Johns Hopkins University Press, 2001.
- Kraybill, Donald B., Karen M. Johnson-Weiner, and Steven M. Nolt.
  *The Amish.* Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013. Retention-rate
  figures cited in §B.3.5.
- Pennings, Ray, et al. *Cardus Education Survey.* Cardus, 2011,
  2014, and 2018. The 2018 U.S. wave's academic and workplace
  findings cited in §B.3.5 — years of education, advanced-degree
  attainment, unemployment, household income — are drawn from
  *From the Classroom to the Workplace* (2018). The 2018 wave's
  civic, volunteering, and charitable-giving findings cited in
  §B.3.5 are drawn from *Involved and Engaged* (2018). Both reports
  are publicly available at cardus.ca.
