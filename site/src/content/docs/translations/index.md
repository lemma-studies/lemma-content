# Translations

Lemma Press is publishing family discipleship materials in multiple languages so that the same content is available to families across the Arabic-, Hebrew-, Farsi-, and Chinese-speaking world in the language they use at home.

Each language edition is its own published work with its own DOI. Editions cross-reference each other via the `sibling_editions` field in `study.yaml` so a reader landing on any one can navigate to the others.

## Planned first releases

- Arabic
- Hebrew
- Farsi
- Chinese (Simplified and Traditional)

Additional languages may follow as translator capacity and community need align.

## How translations relate to the theological studies

Both live on the same site under the same imprint (Lemma Press) for a reason: readers who arrive via one may benefit from the other. The two grow together — theological studies deepen exegetical foundations, translations make discipleship material reachable across the languages families actually speak.
