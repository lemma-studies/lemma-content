# By His Stripes We Are Healed

*"But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed."* — Isaiah 53:5 (KJV)

This study examines one of the most debated questions in Christian theology: **Does "by his stripes we are healed" apply to physical healing?**

Using the SCAR Quadrilateral framework (Scripture, Christocentrism, Antiquity, Reality), this work presents every major perspective with its strongest arguments and lets the reader weigh the evidence.

**Chapter 0** is the complete study — a quick, encouraging 6-8 page read that gives the whole picture.
**Chapters 1–5** are the encyclopedic reference for those who want to go deeper.
