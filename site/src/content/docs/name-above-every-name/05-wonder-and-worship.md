# Chapter 5: Wonder and Worship - The Son's Glory and Our Participation

> "To him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood and made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father, to him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen." (Revelation 1:5-6)

## The Stunning Realization

As the full picture emerges, we find ourselves face to face with a truth so staggering it demands nothing less than worship: The eternal Son of God didn't just receive a promotion—he **earned** the divine name itself through perfect obedience, then **shares** that name with us. 

This isn't just a story about Jesus. It's a story about the **transformation of cosmic government** and our **unthinkable destiny** within it.

---

## The Son's Incredible Journey

### What He Always Was

- Eternally divine in nature (John 1:1)
- In the form of God from eternity (Philippians 2:6)
- The radiance of God's glory (Hebrews 1:3)
- Creator and sustainer of all things (Colossians 1:16-17)

### What He Became

- Truly human while remaining fully divine
- Subject to hunger, thirst, fatigue, and death
- Tempted in every way, yet without sin (Hebrews 4:15)
- Obedient unto death, even death on a cross

### What He Earned

- The name above every name (Philippians 2:9)
- The right to universal worship due to YHWH alone
- Cosmic authority over all creation
- Victory over every enemy, including death itself

---

## The Divine Paradox

He **earned through obedience** what was always **his by nature**. 

The eternal Son proved his worthiness to exercise divine authority by perfect submission to the Father's will. The cross wasn't just about our salvation—it was about the Son's **demonstration** of perfect divine character.

---

## The Father's Wise Plan

### Divine Government Through Delegation

The Father's method reveals infinite wisdom:
- **Never abdicates** his ultimate authority
- **Works through** faithful representatives
- **Demonstrates justice** through earned authority
- **Includes others** in divine government

### The Joy of the Father

Like David rejoicing over Solomon's coronation:

> "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who has granted someone to sit on my throne this day, my own eyes seeing it!" (1 Kings 1:48)

**The Father delights in the Son's exaltation because:**
- It vindicates divine justice
- It demonstrates perfect love
- It accomplishes complete redemption
- It reveals the glory of divine character

### The "Until" as Love

The Son's cosmic authority isn't permanent in the current form—it serves redemptive purposes. When all enemies are defeated, when every knee has bowed, when creation is perfectly restored, the authority **transitions** into the eternal "God all in all" configuration.

This isn't loss but **completion**. The mission is accomplished, and divine government reaches its perfect expression.

---

## Our Unthinkable Destiny

### More Than Forgiveness

We often think salvation means our sins are forgiven and we go to heaven when we die. But Scripture reveals something far more magnificent.

### We Receive Divine Names (Revelation 3:12)

- **The Father's name**—participation in divine nature
- **The city's name**—heavenly citizenship and community
- **Christ's new name**—union with his victory and identity

### We Become Name-Bearers

- Representatives of divine authority
- Participants in divine nature (2 Peter 1:4)
- Sharers in Christ's throne (Revelation 3:21)
- Living expressions of divine glory

### The Personal Secret Name (Revelation 2:17)

Each believer receives:
- A unique name known only to themselves and God
- Personal calling and identity in God's eternal plan
- Individual intimacy with the divine
- Victory and overcoming symbolized by the white stone

This reveals that salvation isn't mass-produced but **personally crafted**. God's plan includes your unique participation in divine government.

---

## The New Jerusalem Reality

### Revelation 21-22: Our Eternal Home

**Features:**
- The throne of God **and** of the Lamb (shared divine rule)
- No temple—we have direct access to divine presence
- Our names written in the Lamb's book of life
- Eating from the tree of life
- Reigning forever and ever

We don't just **visit** the divine realm—we **participate** in it.

---

## The Worship Response

### Amazement at the Son

- He who **created** all things **redeemed** all things
- He who was **rich** became **poor** to make us **rich**
- He who was **worshipped** became the **servant** to make us **kings**
- He who **possessed** divine nature **earned** divine authority to **share** it with us

### Gratitude for the Plan

The divine timeline reveals:
- Nothing is accidental—all serves redemptive purposes
- Our suffering has meaning within the cosmic victory
- History is moving toward certain triumph
- We are not spectators but participants in divine government

### Awe at Our Inclusion

We who were:
- **Enemies** become **children**
- **Rebels** become **representatives**
- **Condemned** become **crowned**
- **Nameless** become **name-bearers**

---

## Living in Light of the Timeline

### Current Reality (Phase 4)

We live in the "until" period:
- Christ exercises cosmic authority **now**
- We participate in divine names **now**
- We exercise spiritual authority **now**
- We await complete victory

**This means:**
- Our prayers carry divine authority
- Our lives demonstrate divine character
- Our mission advances divine kingdom
- Our hope rests on certain victory

### Future Glory (Phases 6-7)

When the "until" ends:
- We witness Christ's complete triumph
- We receive our full divine names
- We participate in the eternal "God all in all" configuration
- We reign forever in perfect harmony with divine government

---

## The Call to Wonder

### At Christ's Achievement

The ancient pattern of faithful service → testing → elevation → authority finds its ultimate expression in the eternal Son. But he transcends all types:

- **Perfect** faithfulness where others failed
- **Eternal** nature where others were finite
- **Cosmic** authority where others ruled earthly kingdoms
- **Shared** victory where others kept their glory

### At Divine Love

The Father didn't need to save us. The Son didn't need to earn what was already his. The Spirit didn't need to indwell us. But divine love **chose** to work through costly delegation to include us in divine government.

### At Our Destiny

We are not just **saved from** something—we are **saved to** something unimaginable:
- Bearing divine names
- Sharing the divine throne
- Participating in divine nature
- Reigning in divine authority

The "name above every name" becomes not just Christ's individual possession, but the gateway to divine participation for all who believe.

---

## The Eternal Perspective

From the cross to the crown to the consummation, the timeline reveals that:

- **Divine government** serves **divine love**
- **Cosmic authority** serves **redemptive purposes**
- **Perfect victory** serves **eternal joy**
- **The Son's glory** includes **our participation**

---

## When We See Jesus

We don't just see our Savior. We see:

- The **King of kings** who earned his throne through a cross
- The **Lord of lords** who exercises authority until every enemy is defeated
- The **Name above every name** who shares divine identity with his people
- The eternal **Son** who makes us children through his perfect obedience

---

## The Final Call

Like John on Patmos, when we glimpse this cosmic reality, we can only fall down in worship:

> "To him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing and honor and glory and might forever and ever!" (Revelation 5:13)

But this worship isn't from a distance. We worship as:

- **Name-bearers** who participate in divine nature
- **Throne-sharers** who reign with Christ
- **Children** who call the ultimate Sovereign "Father"
- **Brothers and sisters** of the King of kings himself

---

## The Never-Ending Story

The journey from cross to crown to consummation reveals that the divine plan was never just about rescuing individuals—it was about **transforming cosmic government** to include redeemed humanity in the very administration of divine authority.

Wonder, worship, and an overwhelming sense of our incredible destiny—this is the only appropriate response to seeing Christ as he truly is: the **Name above every name** who earned divine authority to share divine glory with his people forever.

---

## Doxology

To him who:
- **Came forth** from the Father's presence
- **Emptied himself** in incarnation
- **Became obedient** unto death on a cross
- **Was highly exalted** by the Father
- **Received** the name above every name
- **Reigns** until all enemies are defeated
- **Will return** authority to the Father
- **Shares** divine names with his people
- **Prepares** a place in the New Jerusalem
- **Promises** we will reign with him forever

**To him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.**

---

> "Even so, come, Lord Jesus!" (Revelation 22:20)

---

## Epilogue: The Never-Ending Story

The timeline has an end, but the story never does. When "God is all in all" through the exalted Son, when every enemy is defeated, when every tear is wiped away, when death itself is destroyed—then the **eternal** story begins.

We will spend forever discovering new depths of the name above every name, new dimensions of divine glory, new expressions of perfect love. The Son who earned cosmic authority through the cross will forever share that authority with his people.

And we who once were no people will forever be **the people of the Name**—bearers of divine identity, participants in divine nature, sharers in divine government.

The ancient pattern reaches its perfect expression. The faithful Servant becomes the eternal King. And by grace, we become faithful servants who share his throne.

**To him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.**
