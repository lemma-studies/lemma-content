# Chapter 4: The Great Timeline - From Delegation to Consummation

*"He must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet"*

## The Cosmic Calendar

The biblical narrative reveals not just individual salvation, but a **cosmic timeline** of divine government. From the cross to the New Jerusalem, we can trace the phases of Christ's authority and its ultimate destiny.

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Incarnate Glory

> "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God" (John 1:1)

### The Eternal State Before Time

- The Son possesses divine nature eternally
- Perfect fellowship within the Trinity
- Has not yet received "the name above every name"
- No cosmic governmental mission yet undertaken

### The Divine Plan Formed

- Ephesians 1:4: "Chosen us in him before the foundation of the world"
- 1 Peter 1:20: "Foreknown before the foundation of the world"
- The redemptive timeline planned in eternity

---

## Phase 2: Incarnation and Humiliation

> "Though he was in the form of God, did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped, but emptied himself" (Philippians 2:6-7)

### The Great Descent

- Divine glory veiled in human flesh
- Subject to human limitations and suffering
- Perfect obedience under the Law
- Faithful unto death, even death on a cross

### The Crucial Pattern

Following the ancient template—faithful service through testing before elevation to authority.

---

## Phase 3: Exaltation and Cosmic Authority

> "Therefore God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name" (Philippians 2:9)

### What Jesus Receives

- The divine name itself (likely YHWH)
- Cosmic lordship over all creation
- The right to receive worship due to God alone
- Authority as **reward** for cross-obedience

### The Father's Invitation (Psalm 110:1)

> "The LORD says to my Lord: 'Sit at my right hand **until** I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.'"

**Key Elements:**
- The **session** at the Father's right hand
- The **"until"** factor—this has a specific purpose and endpoint
- The **promise** of complete victory over all enemies

---

## Phase 4: Present Reign - "The Until Period"

> "He must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet" (1 Corinthians 15:25)

### Current Reality

- Christ exercises delegated cosmic authority **now**
- Progressive subjugation of all opposing powers
- Every knee bowing, every tongue confessing (Philippians 2:10-11)
- The church participating in divine names and authority

### Daniel's Vision Unfolding (Daniel 2:34-35)

- The stone "cut without hands" striking earthly kingdoms
- Gradually growing to fill the whole earth
- All human governmental systems being displaced
- The mountain filling all earth

### Characteristics of This Age

- Spiritual warfare and victory
- Gospel advancing to all nations
- Saints participating in divine authority
- Progressive defeat of enemies
- The "already but not yet" tension

---

## Phase 5: Final Victory

> "The last enemy to be destroyed is death" (1 Corinthians 15:26)

### Complete Subjugation

- Absolute and total victory achieved
- No remaining rebellion anywhere in creation
- Death itself conquered and eliminated
- Perfect and universal submission accomplished

### Revelation's Final Battles

- Satan's final rebellion crushed (Revelation 20:7-10)
- Great White Throne judgment (Revelation 20:11-15)
- Complete and final justice executed
- All things subjected to Christ

### The Completion Moment

When absolutely everything is subjected—then and only then does the "until" end.

---

## Phase 6: The Great Transition

> "When all things are subjected to him, then the Son himself will also be subjected to him who put all things in subjection under him, that God may be all in all" (1 Corinthians 15:28)

### The Stunning Climax

- Jesus returns cosmic authority to the Father
- Not defeat or diminishment, but **mission accomplished**
- The purpose of the "until" period is fulfilled
- Authority transitions from delegated to... what?

### The Father's Exception (1 Corinthians 15:27)

> "But when it says, 'all things are put in subjection,' it is plain that he is excepted who put all things in subjection under him."

The Father was never subject to the Son's cosmic authority—he remained the source and destination throughout.

### What This Transition Means

**Not:**
- The Son losing his divine nature
- Return to pre-incarnate relationships exactly
- Dissolution of the Son's role or identity
- End of the Son's glory

**But:**
- Mission completion and transition
- New configuration of divine government
- Perfect fulfillment of redemptive purposes
- Beginning of eternal "God all in all" state

---

## Phase 7: The New Eternal Order

> "The throne of God and of the Lamb" (Revelation 22:1, 3)

### The New Jerusalem Vision

- **One throne, not two**—shared divine rule
- **No temple**—direct divine presence everywhere
- **No sun or moon**—God's glory and the Lamb provide all light
- **River of life**—flowing from the unified divine throne

### What "God All in All" Looks Like

**Not the disappearance of the Son's role, but:**
- A **new configuration** of divine government
- Perfect unity with maintained distinctions
- Direct unmediated divine presence
- Complete restoration and new creation

### The Names Fulfilled

- Believers bear full divine names (Revelation 3:12)
- Individual secret names revealed (Revelation 2:17)
- Christ's new name disclosed
- Perfect participation in divine nature achieved

---

## The Divine Governmental Wisdom

### Why This Timeline?

**The Father's Strategy:**
- Works through **delegated authority** to accomplish redemption
- Demonstrates divine justice through the Son's earned victory
- Includes believers as **participants**, not just spectators
- Reveals divine love through self-sacrificial government

**The Son's Willing Submission:**
- **Earns** what he always possessed by nature
- **Demonstrates** perfect love and obedience
- **Accomplishes** complete cosmic victory
- **Returns** authority in triumphant completion

---

## The Pattern Fulfilled

All the Old Testament types find their ultimate expression:

**Greater than Joseph:** Not just saving from famine, but conquering death
**Greater than David:** Not just earthly kingdom, but cosmic rule
**Greater than Solomon:** Not just inherited wisdom, but wisdom incarnate

But unlike the human representatives, Christ's authority serves a specific **redemptive mission** with an **eschatological endpoint**.

---

## The Timeline's Personal Impact

### For Believers Today (Phase 4)

We live in the "until" period:
- We participate in Christ's present authority
- Bear divine names and exercise divine power
- Live in progressive victory
- Await the completion and our full transformation

### For Creation

- All things moving toward perfect subjugation
- Evil being systematically defeated
- Divine government replacing human rebellion
- History has a direction and destiny

### For the Future

- Complete victory is certain
- Our participation in divine nature will be perfected
- The timeline culminates in unimaginable glory
- "God all in all" through the exalted Son

---

## The Cosmic Progression Summary

```
Phase 1: Pre-Incarnate → Eternal divine nature, no cosmic mission yet
Phase 2: Incarnation → Divine glory veiled, perfect obedience
Phase 3: Exaltation → Receives "the name," cosmic authority
Phase 4: Present Reign → "Until" period, progressive victory
Phase 5: Final Victory → All enemies defeated, including death
Phase 6: Great Transition → Authority returned to Father
Phase 7: Eternal Order → "God all in all," new configuration
```

---

## Key Insights

### The "Until" Factor

- Christ's reign is **purposeful**, not merely endless
- Serves specific **redemptive mission**
- Has **eschatological endpoint**
- Transitions to eternal configuration

### Dynamic Divine Government

Not static hierarchy, but **progression**:
**Delegation → Victory → Completion → Transformation**

### The Father's Constant Role

- Never subjected to the Son
- Always the source
- Ultimate destination
- Works through the Son's delegated authority

### Believer Participation

- Not spectators but participants
- Share in divine names and authority
- Experience progressive transformation
- Await complete fulfillment
