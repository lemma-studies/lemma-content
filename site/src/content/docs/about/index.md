Lemma is a collection of exegetical studies and theological research, each built on the **SCAR Quadrilateral** framework.

## The SCAR Quadrilateral

Every study in this library examines its topic through four lenses:

1. **Scripture** (highest authority) --- Comprehensive analysis of all relevant biblical passages, with attention to the original Hebrew and Greek
2. **Christocentrism** --- What did Jesus teach and demonstrate? His words, actions, and priorities
3. **Antiquity** (pre-325 AD) --- How did the earliest Christians understand and practice this? Patristic writings, rabbinic sources, and early church documents
4. **Reality** --- What does lived experience tell us? How do believers across traditions actually encounter this today?

## How to Read

Each study follows a consistent pattern:

- **Chapter 0 / Executive Summary** is the complete, accessible overview --- read it first
- **Chapters 1--5+** provide encyclopedic depth for those who want to go deeper
- **Appendices** contain word studies, source collections, and cross-references

Every major theological position is presented with its **strongest arguments**, as its best advocates would present it. The reader weighs the evidence.
