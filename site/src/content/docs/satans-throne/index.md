**The question:** What is "Satan's seat" (or "Satan's throne") in Revelation 2:12-13, and where exactly was it? Why did Jesus identify Pergamum specifically as the place "where Satan dwelleth," and who was Antipas who was martyred there?

**Key Scripture:** Revelation 2:12-13

> "And to the angel of the church in Pergamos write; These things saith he which hath the sharp sword with two edges; I know thy works, and where thou dwellest, even where Satan's seat is: and thou holdest fast my name, and hast not denied my faith, even in those days wherein Antipas was my faithful martyr, who was slain among you, where Satan dwelleth."
> --- Revelation 2:12-13 (KJV)

| **Scripture** | The Greek *ho thronos tou Satana* ("the throne of Satan") points to a specific, identifiable location in Pergamum. The word *thronos* (throne) implies an established seat of authority, not a vague spiritual presence. Jesus uses both "where Satan's seat is" and "where Satan dwelleth" --- a double emphasis unique among the seven churches. Antipas (*Antipas ho martus mou ho pistos mou*) is the only individual named as a martyr in Revelation. |

**Major views:**
- **The Great Altar of Zeus:** The massive altar on the Pergamon acropolis, described by ancient writers and rediscovered by German archaeologists in the 1870s, is the literal "throne of Satan"
- **The imperial cult center:** Pergamum was the first city in Asia Minor to receive permission to build a temple to a living Roman emperor (Augustus, 29 BC), making it the provincial seat of emperor worship
- **The Asklepion:** The healing sanctuary of Asklepios, whose serpent symbol directly evoked Satan, drew pilgrims from across the Roman world
- **Convergence view:** "Satan's throne" refers to the entire religious-political complex of Pergamum --- the city was uniquely saturated with pagan cult, imperial worship, and serpent veneration
