# Chapter 3: The Sacred Names - Participation in Divine Nature

*"I will write on him the name of my God... and my own new name"*

## More Than Labels

In the ancient world, names carried power. They represented essence, authority, and relationship. To bear someone's name meant to participate in their identity and exercise their authority. When Scripture speaks of divine names, it's revealing something far more profound than mere titles.

---

## Old Testament Foundations

### The Revelation of YHWH (Exodus 3:13-15)

> "God said to Moses, 'I AM WHO I AM.' And he said, 'Say this to the people of Israel: I AM has sent me to you... This is my name forever, and thus I am to be remembered throughout all generations.'"

**The Tetragrammaton (YHWH):**
- The personal, covenant name of God
- Represents **eternal self-existence** and **faithfulness**
- Too sacred to pronounce—called "The Name" (Hashem)
- Foundation of all covenant relationships

### Names Revealing Divine Character

**Exodus 34:6-7: The Name Proclaimed**

> "The LORD passed before him and proclaimed, 'The LORD, The LORD, a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness...'"

**Each divine name unveils another facet of God's nature:**
- **El Shaddai** - God Almighty, covenant power
- **Adonai** - Lord, sovereign authority
- **El Elyon** - Most High God, supreme rule

### The Power of Divine Names

**David facing Goliath (1 Samuel 17:45):**

> "I come to you in the name of the LORD of hosts"

- Authority flows through the name
- Speaking and acting with divine backing
- Representing God's power and presence

---

## The New Testament Revolution

### Jesus' Name Carries Divine Power

**Acts 3:6 - The Lame Man Healed:**

> "In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk!"

**Acts 4:12 - Exclusive Salvation:**

> "There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

**Acts 19:13-16 - The Seven Sons of Sceva:**

The evil spirits recognize Jesus' name but reject unauthorized users: 

> "Jesus I know, and Paul I recognize, but who are you?"

### Prayer in Jesus' Name

**John 14:13-14, 16:23-24:**

> "Whatever you ask in my name, this I will do... If you ask me anything in my name, I will do it."

Not a magical formula, but authority delegation—praying with Christ's authority and according to his will.

---

## The Name Transformation Pattern

### Isaiah 62:1-4 - Jerusalem's New Names

> "You will be called by a new name that the mouth of the LORD will bestow... No longer will they call you Deserted, or name your land Desolate. But you will be called Hephzibah [My Delight is in her], and your land Beulah [Married]."

**The Pattern:**
- Old names reflect broken condition
- New names reflect restored relationship
- Transformation brings new identity
- God himself gives the new names

### Individual Name Changes

- **Jacob to Israel** - from "Supplanter" to "He who strives with God"
- **Simon to Peter** - from "Reed" to "Rock"
- **Saul to Paul** - from persecutor to apostle

Names change when identity transforms through divine encounter.

---

## Revelation's Multiple Names

### Christ's Layered Identity (Revelation 19:11-16)

**Three distinct names revealed:**

#### 1. The Secret Name (v.12)

> "He has a name written that no one knows but himself"

- The incomprehensible divine essence
- Mystery that belongs to God alone
- Infinite depths beyond creature understanding
- Even in glorified state, aspects remain hidden

#### 2. "The Word of God" (v.13)

> "The name by which he is called"

- His revealed identity and function
- Divine communication and revelation incarnate
- The means by which God speaks to creation
- Connects to John 1:1—"In the beginning was the Word"

#### 3. "King of Kings and Lord of Lords" (v.16)

Written on robe and thigh:
- Cosmic governmental authority
- Supreme sovereignty over all earthly powers
- The culmination of his exalted status
- Public declaration of ultimate rule

---

## Believers Receiving Divine Names

### Revelation 3:12 - The Promise to Philadelphia

> "I will write on him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem... and my own new name."

**Three names written on overcomers:**

1. **The Father's name** - Participation in divine nature
2. **The city's name** (New Jerusalem) - Heavenly citizenship and community
3. **Christ's new name** - Union with his victory and identity

### Revelation 14:1 - The 144,000

> "They had his name and his Father's name written on their foreheads."

Both the Son's name **and** the Father's name—complete divine identity sharing.

### The Believer's Secret Name (Revelation 2:17)

> "I will give him a white stone, with a new name written on the stone that no one knows except the one who receives it."

**The Mystery of Individual Names:**
- Each believer receives a **unique name** known only to themselves and God
- Suggests **personalized relationship** and **individual calling**
- The **white stone** may represent acquittal, victory, or admission
- Parallels Christ's secret name—intimate divine knowledge

---

## The Divine Name Economy

### The Father's Role

- **Source** of all divine names and authority
- **Gives** the name above every name to the Son
- **Writes** his name on believers
- **Remains** the ultimate reference point

### The Son's Role

- **Receives** YHWH as reward for cross-obedience
- **Bears** multiple functional names (Word, King of kings, etc.)
- **Shares** divine names with believers
- **Mediates** divine identity to his people

### The Believers' Role

- **Receive** divine names as participation in divine nature
- **Bear** God's authority through his name
- **Exercise** divine power through Christ's name
- **Await** fuller name revelation in the eternal state

---

## Participation, Not Just Forgiveness

The staggering truth emerges: salvation isn't just **forgiveness** but **participation**. We don't just get our sins erased—we get **God's names written on us**. We become bearers of divine identity, sharers in divine nature (2 Peter 1:4).

### What It Means to Bear Divine Names

**2 Chronicles 7:14:**

> "If my people who are called by my name..."

**Jeremiah 15:16:**

> "For I am called by your name, O LORD, God of hosts."

**Being called by God's name means:**
- Covenant relationship and belonging
- Authority to act as God's representatives
- Participation in divine mission
- Identity transformation

## The Progression of Divine Names Through Scripture

### Old Testament: Names Reveal Divine Character

- **YHWH** - Covenant faithfulness and eternal existence
- **El Shaddai** - Almighty power and provision
- **Adonai** - Sovereign lordship
- **El Elyon** - Most High God

### New Testament: Names Reveal Divine Mission

- **Jesus** (Yeshua) - "YHWH saves"
- **Christ** (Messiah) - "Anointed One"
- **Lord** (Kyrios) - Divine authority, often translating YHWH
- **The Word** - Divine revelation and creative power

### Revelation: Names Reveal Divine Completion

- **King of kings and Lord of lords** - Ultimate cosmic authority
- **The Word of God** - Perfect divine revelation
- **Secret name** - Incomprehensible divine mystery
- **New name** - Eschatological identity

---

## The Christological Connection

### Evidence for YHWH as the Received Name

**Linguistic Evidence:**
- "**The** name above every name" (definite article suggests specific, known name)
- In Jewish context, "The Name" always meant the Tetragrammaton
- Romans 10:13 applies "call on the name of YHWH" to calling on Jesus

**Theological Evidence:**
- Only YHWH would truly be "above every name"
- Explains universal worship (every knee bows to YHWH in Isaiah 45:23)
- Accounts for Jesus receiving worship due to God alone

### The Multiple Names Theory

**Different Names for Different Functions:**

- **YHWH** - Essential divine identity received at exaltation
- **The Word of God** - Revelatory function and mission
- **King of kings and Lord of lords** - Cosmic governmental authority
- **Secret name** - Incomprehensible divine essence
- **New name** - Eschatological identity for eternal state

## Believers and Divine Names

### Receiving Multiple Divine Names (Revelation 3:12)

**The Father's Name:**
- Participation in divine nature (2 Peter 1:4)
- Covenant relationship and belonging
- Authority to act as God's representatives

**The City's Name (New Jerusalem):**
- Citizenship in heavenly kingdom
- Participation in divine community
- Identity with God's eternal purposes

**Christ's New Name:**
- Union with the exalted Christ
- Sharing in his victory and authority
- Participation in his eschatological identity

### Individual Secret Names (Revelation 2:17)

**Personal Relationship:**
- Unique calling and identity in God's plan
- Intimate knowledge between God and individual
- Personal victory and overcoming

**The White Stone:**
- Ancient symbol of acquittal in courts
- Admission token to feasts
- Victory prize in competitions
- New identity and acceptance

## Synthesis: The Divine Name Economy

### Current Age

- Christ bears the divine name and cosmic authority
- Believers bear preliminary divine names
- Secret aspects remain hidden
- Progressive revelation and participation

### Coming Age

- Christ's new name revealed
- Believers receive full divine name inscription
- Personal secret names disclosed
- Perfect participation in divine nature achieved

### Eternal State

- "God all in all" configuration
- Complete divine name sharing
- Perfect union while maintaining distinct personalities
- The name economy reaches its consummation

---

## The Staggering Reality

The "name above every name" becomes not just Christ's individual possession, but the gateway for believers to participate in divine identity itself. The Son who received the divine name now shares it with his people.

This progression reveals that divine names are not merely labels but represent **participatory reality**—sharing in God's own nature, authority, and eternal purposes through covenant relationship and union with Christ.
