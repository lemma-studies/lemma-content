# Meeting Structure: Lord's Supper and Participatory Worship

## Goal

Create a comprehensive book-length work using the **SCAR Quadrilateral** (Scripture, Christocentrism, Antiquity, Reality) framework to address how Christian meetings should be structured to honor both:
1. **Reverent, focused Lord's Supper** (remembering Christ's death)
2. **Open participatory worship** (mutual edification, teaching, doctrine, spiritual gifts)

**Book Structure:** 2-3 page TL;DR executive summary + main analysis chapters + extensive appendices with original sources

## Context

**Current Situation in Brethren Context:**
- Weekly meeting is "kind of open" around the Lord's Supper
- People are free to speak BUT must focus on the Lord's Supper
- Doctrinal teaching (e.g., doctrine of healing) is not welcome during Lord's Supper time
- After meeting: snacks, singing, testimonies, prayer requests
- Still not a place for doctrinal sharing/learnings in "open meeting way"
- There's a prepared lesson with discussion
- Some want another segment ("two meetings") where they can "exercise" gifts and share doctrinal things

**The Tension:**
- Need for focused, reverent remembrance of Christ's death
- Need for open participatory teaching and mutual edification
- How to honor both without diluting either?

### Key Insight

The question isn't whether to have Lord's Supper OR participatory worship, but **how to structure meetings to honor both** without diluting either.

### Potential Solutions to Explore

1. **Two-Part Meeting:** Focused Lord's Supper + Open participatory worship
2. **Separate Meetings:** Lord's Supper meeting + Teaching/doctrinal meeting
3. **Integrated with Guidelines:** Single meeting with clear boundaries
4. **Sequential Structure:** Lord's Supper first, then participatory worship
