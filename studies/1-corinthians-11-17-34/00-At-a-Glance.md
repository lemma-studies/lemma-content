# 1 Corinthians 11:17-34 — A Passage Study

**What this is:** A passage-particular study of 1 Cor 11:17-34 — the Lord's Supper passage in Paul. Not a question-driven SCAR project; the prior *Meeting Structure* study (2025, archived) already answered the meeting-structure question. This study has a different job: **walk the passage carefully, surface what it opens, map the live positions without forcing one answer**.

**The passage at a glance:**

> "Now in this that I declare unto you I praise you not, that ye come together not for the better, but for the worse." — 1 Corinthians 11:17 (KJV)

**Four-movement shape:**

| Movement | Verses | What Paul is doing |
|----------|--------|--------------------|
| **A. The problem at Corinth** | 17-22 | Diagnosis: their gatherings make things worse |
| **B. The tradition received** | 23-26 | The dominical institution: what Paul received and delivered |
| **C. Worthy participation** | 27-32 | Pastoral consequence: how to come without bringing judgment |
| **D. Practical ordering** | 33-34 | Apostolic regulation: wait for one another; eat at home; the rest comes later |

**The live cruxes:**

- v. 19: "heresies must be… that they which are approved may be made manifest" — what does Paul mean?
- v. 20-22: was the Supper actually a full meal? (yes — see Historical Context)
- vv. 24-25: "this is my body / this cup is the new testament in my blood" — real presence vs. memorialism vs. spiritual presence
- v. 26: "till he come" — Supper as proclamation and anticipation
- v. 27: "unworthily" — what makes participation unworthy?
- v. 29: "discerning the body" — Christ's body? the church body? both?
- v. 30: "many are weak and sickly… and many sleep" — does this still apply today?
- v. 34: "the rest will I set in order when I come" — what's *not* in our text
