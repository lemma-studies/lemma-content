# Trumpet Call: At a Glance

**The core question:** Which comes first — the antichrist's appearance, or the gathering of believers with Jesus?

---

## The Order Question

| Answer | Views Holding This |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Gathering before the antichrist appears** | Pre-tribulation rapture |
| **Antichrist first; gathering before God's final wrath** | Mid-tribulation; Pre-wrath; Partial-rapture (the watchful first company) |
| **Antichrist first; gathering at the single visible return** | Post-tribulation, Historicist, Amillennial, Catholic, Orthodox |
| **Antichrist already past; gathering future** | Preterist |

---

## The Key Biblical Sequence

*An observational note, not a verdict: the sequence below is what the two central texts state on their face. Whether that surface sequence governs the rapture question is precisely what the traditions dispute --- the pre-tribulation counter-readings (an Israel-focused discourse; a Day of the Lord that opens with the church already gathered) are presented at full strength in Chapters 2 and 4.*

> "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened... and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds..."
> --- Matthew 24:29-31 (KJV)

**Jesus's sequence:** tribulation → signs → Son of man comes → angels trumpet → **gather elect**

> "Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and **that man of sin be revealed**, the son of perdition."
> --- 2 Thessalonians 2:3 (KJV)

**Paul's sequence:** falling away first → **man of sin revealed** → then "that day" (Christ's return)

## Major Views on the Order

| View | Order | Antichrist Timeline |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| **Pre-Tribulation** | Gathering → Antichrist | Antichrist appears AFTER rapture |
| **Mid-Tribulation** | Antichrist → Gathering | Antichrist at midpoint, then gathering |
| **Post-Tribulation** | Antichrist → Gathering | Gathering at Christ's return, after tribulation |
| **Historicist** | Antichrist already here | Papacy is antichrist; gathering at Christ's return |
| **Amillennial** | Antichrist → Gathering | Antichrist future; gathering at single return |

---

## What the Earliest Christians Believed

- **No gathering placed before the antichrist's appearance** in the first 1,700 years of church history (a handful of disputed "removal" texts exist --- see Chapter 3; absence of explicit pre-trib language is an argument from silence, not by itself a disproof)
- Augustine (c. 426): Christ "will not come to judge quick and dead unless Antichrist, His adversary, first come" (*City of God* 20.19)
- Catholic Catechism (1992): "Before Christ's second coming the Church must pass through a final trial"
- Eastern Orthodox: Same sequence — antichrist first, then Christ's return

---

## Common Ground

All views agree:
- Christ will return visibly
- The dead will be raised
- Believers will be glorified
- The antichrist will be destroyed
- Final judgment will occur
- We are to watch and be ready (the partial-rapture view alone grades the outcome of watchfulness --- see Chapter 4)

---

## The Core Question

The debate is not mainly about tribulation — it's about **order**:

1. Does the gathering happen **before** the antichrist is revealed? (Pre-tribulation)
2. Does the antichrist appear first, with the gathering **during his career but before God's final wrath**? (Mid-tribulation; pre-wrath)
3. Or does the gathering come **at Christ's single visible return**, after the antichrist's course has run? (Post-tribulation and the single-return traditions)

---

**For every believer:** Watch, be ready, love one another — regardless of which view on timing you hold.
